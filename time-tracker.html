Frequently Asked Questions

What does "offline-first" mean?

The app keeps your data on your phone and works from that copy, so timers, entries and reports work without a connection. Changes are sent to our servers and to your other devices when you are back online. You do not have to do anything to make it sync.

Where does my data live?

On your device, for offline use, and in our cloud database (Supabase) so it can sync between your devices. If you connect Google Calendar, the calendars and events Time Tracker creates live in your Google account. We never sell your data. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How does calendar sync work?

Calendar sync is a Pro feature, and it is one way: from Time Tracker to Google. Each client you choose to sync gets its own calendar, and finished entries are added to it by our server, even when the app is closed. Changes you make in Google Calendar are not copied back into the app. Time Tracker can only create its own calendars and manage the events in them. It cannot see or change any other calendar in your Google account.

How do AI summaries work?

AI summaries are a Pro feature, and they are only written when you ask for one. No API key is needed: the summary is written by Anthropic on our behalf. If you prefer, you can use your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter key instead, and the request goes straight from your device to that provider.
